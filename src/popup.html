    Streaming

    Chat beta - off by default

    Chat (and the free-for-all override) can only be switched on from the toolbar popup: click the extension's icon. Here they can only be switched off.

    Before chat turns on
    • Your messages, your OpenFront name and clan tag (or "Player" in streamer mode), a throw-away key for that game and a room tag that identifies the game are sent to four public Nostr relays: relay.primal.net, nos.lol, nostr.mom, relay.snort.social. They are third parties and see your IP address.
    • The chat is public: anyone connected to those relays can read the room, and nobody can promise a relay keeps nothing. Your name is announced to the room while chat is on, even if you do not type (not while it is paused in a free-for-all: then the tab leaves the room).
    • In team games, with "Encrypted team channel" on (the default): from the start of the game and about every 45 s, with no click, your key tells the room which player slot of that game it is, which teammates' keys you verified, and any key that claimed to be you. Pressing Verify publishes a handshake naming that teammate's key and both player slots. Team messages are encrypted to teammates verified through the game - by you, or through a chain of teammates who verified each other (up to about eight steps); anyone in that chain can therefore let someone else read them. Only the text is encrypted: the room sees who sent each message, to which keys, when, and roughly how long it is. In streamer mode too, anyone in the game can match your slot to your name.
    • A script running in OpenFront's page (its ad scripts included) could fake what the game shows the extension, get its own key verified as your teammate once you press Verify on it, and then read the team channel. The same goes for the page of any teammate in that chain, without any click of yours. The extension cannot prevent that.
    • Names in the public room are not verified. Be decent; do not share personal information. You can mute anyone, and report abuse from the chat panel.
    Privacy policy

    Always open in the lobby, after the game and during team games. No server of ours: the relays see your IP address and what you send; other players see your name, your text and a throw-away key for that game (in team games also the player slot it claims and whom it verified), never your IP address.
