Before chat turns on
  • Your messages, your OpenFront name and clan tag (or "Player" in streamer mode) and a throw-away key for that game are sent to four public Nostr relays: relay.primal.net, nos.lol, nostr.mom, relay.snort.social. They are third parties and see your IP address.
  • The chat is public: anyone connected to those relays can read the room, and nobody can promise a relay keeps nothing. Your name is announced to the room while chat is on, even if you do not type.
  • Names are not verified. Be decent; do not share personal information. You can mute anyone, and report abuse from the chat panel.
Privacy policy

Chat is always open in the lobby, after the game and during team games. In a free-for-all it pauses while you are alive: OpenFront's terms forbid outside channels for coordinating there. Turning this on is your call and your risk.
