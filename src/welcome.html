To show ranks, player names are looked up at a third-party service

  • The player names shown in your lobby and game - including your own OpenFront username - clan tags, and any name you look up in the dashboard are sent to api.ofstats.io, an independent community statistics service for public OpenFront games, to fetch each player's public statistics.
  • When a game ends, that game's id is sent to OpenFront's own public API (api.openfront.io) to read the public record of the game for the recap. A game link you check in the Observer page sends its id to OpenFront's own game servers, to see whether it is running.
  • Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to those services. They are run by third parties under their own policies.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer of this extension - there is no developer server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Settings, a short-lived cache and your session results stay in this browser (with the Steam companion launcher: in a file on this computer).

Without this, themes and website layouts still work; ranks, your stats card on the front page, the dashboard and the recap (with its timelapse) stay off. You can change your mind any time from the extension's popup.

Chat is separate, and off

The optional chat sends your messages through public Nostr relays and is public to everyone in the room. It stays off until you switch it on in the popup, where it asks for its own agreement.
